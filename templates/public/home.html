A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Getting started with KenadHr? Here are a few things worth knowing.

Who is KenadHr for?

KenadHr brings HR teams, company administrators, managers and employees into a connected workspace. Your role and permissions determine the tools available to you.

How do I get an account?

{% if company_registration_enabled %} Company administrators can register their company and create a workspace. Employees should ask their HR team to create their account, then sign in. {% else %} Ask your company’s HR team or administrator to set up your staff account. Then use Sign in to access your workspace. This homepage does not create new company accounts. {% endif %}

Can one account access multiple companies?

No. Each staff account belongs to one company. Your company is determined by your account, so you do not need to choose or switch companies when you sign in.

Does payroll support Ghanaian Cedis?

Yes. Your administrator can select Ghanaian Cedis (GHS) and other supported African currencies in the company’s payroll settings. Currency selection controls display; it is not automatic currency conversion or tax compliance.

What if I forget my password?

Choose Forgot password on the sign-in page and use your registered email address. If the reset email does not arrive, contact your HR administrator to check your account and company email settings.
